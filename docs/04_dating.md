# 04 — Dating

**User constraint honored here:** the 1988 radiocarbon result is documented as an objection and is **excluded from the LICA posterior**. WAXS / X-ray cellulose dating is included. Alternative chemical and mechanical dates are included. None of these alternative clocks is as mature as AMS radiocarbon. That fact is stated, not hidden.

## The 1988 result (documented, then set aside)

Damon et al., *Nature* 337 (1989) 611–615.

- Labs: Arizona, Oxford, Zurich.
- Combined calendar interval: **1260–1390 CE** at 95%.
- Sample: one corner of the cloth, Raes / C-14 area near the feet of the frontal image.

This is the single most-cited argument that the Shroud is medieval. A serious package lists it first.

## Why sindonologists dispute the sample, not the counters

Rogers, *Thermochimica Acta* 425 (2005) 189–194. DOI 10.1016/j.tca.2004.09.029.

- C-14 corner uniquely coated with a yellow-brown plant gum containing dye lakes (madder).
- Pyrolysis-MS + microscopy: the dated threads are chemically unlike the main cloth.
- Rogers’ conclusion: the dated sample was not part of the original weave (medieval repair / splice hypothesis).
- Vanillin test: main-cloth lignin is vanillin-negative, like very old linens (Dead Sea area comparanda). Medieval Holland cloth and Raes threads are vanillin-positive.
- Kinetic estimate from vanillin loss: **1,300–3,000 years** for the main cloth.

Casabianca, Marinelli, Pernagallo, Torrisi, *Archaeometry* 61 (2019) 1223–1231. DOI 10.1111/arcm.12467.

- British Museum raw data released after legal request, 2017.
- Inter-lab and intra-lab dates for Sample 1 (the Shroud) fail homogeneity; Ward-and-Wilson agreement index reported below the conventional 60% threshold.
- Conclusion of the authors: the 1988 combination procedure should be reconsidered; a new dating protocol is warranted.

Counter-literature exists (see `07_objections.md`). LICA does not pretend the heterogeneity paper “cancels” *Nature* 1989. It shows the published medieval date is not a clean, uncontested measurement of the whole cloth.

## WAXS / X-ray dating (included)

De Caro, Sibillano, Lassandro, Giannini, Fanti, *Heritage* 5 (2022) 860–870. DOI 10.3390/heritage5020047.

- Method: Wide-Angle X-ray Scattering of cellulose crystal degradation.
- Sample: ~0.5 × 1 mm thread near the 1988 / Raes corner.
- Integrated WAXS profile reported **fully compatible** with Masada linen historically dated **55–74 CE**.
- Natural-aging model yields a ~2,000-year cloth **if** mean secular storage was about 20.0–22.5 °C and 55–75% relative humidity during the undocumented centuries.
- Authors note that matching the 1988 date would require implausible long-term storage near Earth’s maximum recorded room temperatures.
- They argue the 1532 fire did not dominate the WAXS aging signal.

**Honest limits**

- One laboratory group.
- Calibration set is small.
- Result is **conditional on the storage-temperature history** — a parameter nobody measured for 13 undocumented centuries.
- The sample is near the same corner Rogers says is anomalous. That cuts both ways: either WAXS dated repaired material and still got first-century-like degradation (awkward for the repair hypothesis), or the corner is original enough for WAXS to be speaking about the cloth (awkward for Rogers’ splice). LICA records the tension instead of picking a winner in secret.

## Fanti multi-clock suite (included, lower weight)

Fanti, Baraldi, Basso, Tinti, *Vibrational Spectroscopy* 67 (2013) 61–70 — FTIR / Raman calibration on dated flax.

Fanti, Malfi, Crosilla, *MATEC Web of Conferences* 36 (2015) 01001:

| Clock | Point estimate |
|---|---|
| FTIR/ATR | ~250 BC ± 400 yr |
| Raman | ~30 AD (paper text) / ~200 BC ± 500 yr (related writeups) |
| Mechanical (break strength, modulus, loss factor) | ~260 AD |
| Combined (2015 paper) | **90 AD ± 200 yr (95%)** |

Limits: calibration samples are scarce; environmental history again confounds cellulose degradation; independent labs have not rerun the full suite on new Shroud fibers under a locked protocol. Weight = `SUPPORTED`, not `ESTABLISHED`.

## How LICA uses dates

Under the user rule:

- 1988 C-14 is **not** multiplied into the posterior.
- WAXS + vanillin + Fanti form a **compatible-with-first-century cluster**.
- The cluster is not a replacement AMS date. It is permission for L5 to consider a first-century named man without immediately being ruled out by a clock.

If a future multi-lab AMS of documented *main-cloth* threads returns 1260–1390 again, LICA’s identity inference collapses regardless of image physics. That is the correct falsifier. It has not been run.
